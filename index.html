प्रमुख प्रचालन सेवाएं • Core Polar Operations Modules

Integrated scientific logistical subsystems deployed across the Indian Antarctic Sector.

Station Telemetry

Real-time meteorology, barometric pressure, wind velocity, and life-support habitat status at Maitri, Bharati, and Dakshin Ice Camp.

Polar Vessel Tracking

Heavy ice-class cargo vessel routes from Cape Town and Goa to Prydz Bay, monitoring sea-ice thickness and berthing logistics.

Cryogenic Fuel Logistics

Automated burn-rate computation for Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) and generator diesel to guarantee survival through the polar winter.

SAR & Emergency Distress

Direct satellite-linked emergency beacon monitoring with automated emergency airlift and medical triage response protocols.

National Polar Science & Logistics Framework

The Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica (ISEA-44) coordinates ongoing scientific research across Maitri, Bharati, and high-plateau Dakshin Camp. Mission controllers oversee vessel routing, fuel rationing, cryogenic life support, and SATCOM downlinks under strict government protocols.

Mission controllers can review real-time AIS maritime transponders, check station life-support reserves, or explore the tactical geospatial corridor ↗ connecting Indian departure ports with Antarctica.
